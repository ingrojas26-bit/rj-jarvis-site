What RJ Jarvis does

One place to shape your content.

RJ Jarvis works as a desktop tool. Your plans, publishing queue and generated videos are managed in your local workspace.

01 / PLAN

Plan with purpose

Organize topics, formats, platforms and planned dates before producing a video.

02 / CREATE

Create and manage video

Turn a selected idea into a video, keep the resulting MP4 and track its status.

03 / REVIEW

Approve before sending

Review your title, caption, hashtags, file and privacy setting. A separate confirmation is required to start a TikTok upload.

Official connections

Connected on your terms.

RJ Jarvis can connect to compatible platforms through their official APIs. The current TikTok test integration uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. Other platform packages can be prepared locally; their publishing APIs are not connected in this version.

How TikTok access works →

Your confirmation is the final step

RJ Jarvis does not publish to TikTok merely because a video is ready. You first review the destination account and publishing details, then give a separate confirmation to initiate the private test upload.

RJ Jarvis is an independent tool. TikTok does not sponsor, endorse or own RJ Jarvis.
